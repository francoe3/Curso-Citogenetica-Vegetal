Bibliografia básica

As dez leituras abaixo cobrem morfologia cromossômica, assimetria cariotípica, hibridização in situ, evolução cromossômica e poliploidia. Os links DOI levam às páginas oficiais dos trabalhos; o acesso ao texto completo pode depender da instituição.

  1. Levan, A., Fredga, K. & Sandberg, A. A. (1964). Nomenclature for centromeric position on chromosomes. Hereditas 52: 201–220. DOI.
  2. Romero Zarco, C. (1986). A new method for estimating karyotype asymmetry. Taxon 35: 526–530. DOI.
  3. Gall, J. G. & Pardue, M. L. (1969). Formation and detection of RNA-DNA hybrid molecules in cytological preparations. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 63: 378–383. DOI.
  4. Wu, F. & Tanksley, S. D. (2010). Chromosomal evolution in the plant family Solanaceae. BMC Genomics 11: 182. DOI. PDF de acesso aberto.
  5. Chiarini, F., Sazatornil, F. & Bernardello, G. (2018). Data reassessment in a phylogenetic context gives insight into chromosome evolution in the giant genus Solanum (Solanaceae). Systematics and Biodiversity 16: 397–416. DOI.
  6. Soltis, D. E. et al. (2009). Polyploidy and angiosperm diversification. American Journal of Botany 96: 336–348. DOI.
  7. Soltis, D. E., Visger, C. J. & Soltis, P. S. (2014). The polyploidy revolution then…and now: Stebbins revisited. American Journal of Botany 101: 1057–1078. DOI.
  8. Soltis, P. S., Marchant, D. B., Van de Peer, Y. & Soltis, D. E. (2015). Polyploidy and genome evolution in plants. Current Opinion in Genetics & Development 35: 119–125. DOI.
  9. Li, Z. et al. (2021). Patterns and Processes of Diploidization in Land Plants. Annual Review of Plant Biology 72: 387–410. DOI.
  10. Heslop-Harrison, J. S. P., Schwarzacher, T. & Liu, Q. (2023). Polyploidy: its consequences and enabling role in plant diversification and evolution. Annals of Botany 131: 1–10. DOI.
